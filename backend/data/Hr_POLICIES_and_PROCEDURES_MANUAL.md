# PART 1: INTRODUCTION
---

## 1.1 COMPANY OVERVIEW
BluePeak Creative Group is a global marketing and creative services agency specializing in brand strategy, digital communication, visual design, multimedia production, and content development. The company collaborates with clients across diverse industries, delivering solutions that enhance brand presence, customer engagement, and market impact.

The organization operates with a multidisciplinary team structure, integrating creative, strategic, and technical competencies to produce high-quality results tailored to client objectives.

## 1.2 MISSION
The mission of BluePeak Creative Group is to provide innovative, results-driven creative solutions that elevate brands and empower clients to communicate effectively in a dynamic and competitive global environment.

The company strives to integrate strategy, creativity, and technology into cohesive, impactful work.

## 1.3 VISION
BluePeak Creative Group aims to become a leading international creative agency recognized for excellence in design, storytelling, digital communication, and collaborative partnerships. The company seeks to build sustainable, long-term relationships with clients through quality, reliability, and innovation.

## 1.4 BUSINESS FOCUS
BluePeak’s core areas of expertise include:
- brand identity and design  
- digital content development  
- marketing strategy and consulting  
- social media management and analytics  
- video production and multimedia storytelling  
- campaign execution and performance analysis

# PART 2: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
---

## 2.1 EMPLOYMENT CATEGORIES
BluePeak Creative Group classifies employees according to regional regulations and operational needs.

Employment categories include:
- full-time employees  
- part-time employees  
- fixed-term contract employees  
- interns and trainees  
- freelancers or consultants  

Each category has specific entitlements governed by regional labor law and internal company policy.

## 2.2 WORKING HOURS
Full-time employees typically work 40 hours per week. Work schedules may vary depending on regional requirements, client needs, and internal workflow structures.

Employees are expected to:
- arrive on time  
- adhere to assigned schedules  
- coordinate with their manager regarding schedule changes  
- communicate planned absences in advance  

## 2.3 OVERTIME AND FLEXIBILITY
Overtime may occur during peak project periods or client deadlines.

Rules:
- overtime must be approved in advance  
- compensation is determined by regional law or policy  
- flexible hours may be allowed depending on workload  

Employees are encouraged to maintain healthy work-life balance.

## 2.4 REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES
Remote work arrangements require:
- manager approval  
- compliance with security policies  
- a stable internet connection  
- a workspace suitable for productive work  

Remote employees must remain available during agreed working hours and actively participate in communication and meetings.

## 2.5 COMPENSATION
Compensation consists of salary, bonuses (where applicable), and additional benefits based on regional practices.

Compensation is determined by:
- role and responsibility  
- market benchmarks  
- employee performance  
- company financial conditions  

Reviews occur periodically.

## 2.6 EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS
Approved business expenses may include:
- travel  
- accommodation  
- meals during business travel  
- production-related costs  
- client-related expenses  
- required software or equipment  

Employees must:
1. obtain approval before incurring expenses  
2. submit receipts  
3. follow accounting guidelines

# PART 4: CODE OF CONDUCT AND PROFESSIONAL EXPECTATIONS
---

## 4.1 CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees must uphold the highest standards of professionalism, ethics, and integrity. Conduct should reflect positively on BluePeak Creative Group and its clients.

Employees must:
- act ethically and responsibly  
- follow all company policies and legal requirements  
- maintain confidentiality  
- use company resources appropriately  
- avoid conflicts of interest  
- communicate respectfully and professionally  

Violation of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action.

## 4.2 PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR
Professional expectations include:
- punctuality  
- reliability  
- timely communication  
- meeting deadlines  
- constructive feedback  
- collaboration with colleagues  
- respectful interaction with clients  

Unprofessional or disruptive behavior is not tolerated.

## 4.3 ETHICAL STANDARDS
Employees must:
- produce original work  
- credit sources properly  
- avoid plagiarism  
- report inaccuracies or mistakes  
- not engage in fraudulent activity  

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed immediately.

## 4.4 ANTI-HARASSMENT AND ANTI-DISCRIMINATION
The company prohibits:
- verbal, visual, or physical harassment  
- discrimination based on protected characteristics  
- retaliation against individuals who report concerns  

Employees must report misconduct immediately to HR or a manager.

## 4.5 WORKPLACE RELATIONSHIPS
Close personal or romantic relationships between supervisors and direct reports must be disclosed to HR so conflicts of interest can be avoided.

## 4.6 SUBSTANCE USE
Employees must not:
- work under the influence of alcohol or drugs  
- consume alcohol during work hours unless at approved events  
- possess illegal substances on company premises  

## 4.7 DRESS CODE
BluePeak applies a “professional casual” dress code.

Employees must ensure:
- clothing is clean and presentable  
- attire is appropriate for client interactions  
- production staff follow safety attire rules  

## 4.8 COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
Employees must:
- maintain professionalism in email and messaging  
- respond within reasonable timeframes  
- avoid inappropriate or offensive language  
- confirm recipients before sending sensitive information  

Client communication must be clear, accurate, and timely.

## 4.9 SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY
Employees must not:
- disclose confidential information  
- comment publicly on behalf of the company without approval  
- post derogatory statements about colleagues or clients  

Company social media accounts must follow brand guidelines.

## 4.10 USE OF COMPANY BRAND AND ASSETS
Employees must follow brand guidelines and use BluePeak materials only for authorized business purposes.

# PART 6: LEAVE POLICIES
---

## 6.1 PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year.  
Part-time employees receive prorated PTO.

### ACCRUAL
PTO accrues monthly beginning on the employee’s first day.

### PTO REQUESTS
Employees must:
- submit PTO requests through the internal system  
- provide reasonable notice (recommended two weeks)  
- coordinate with their manager for coverage  

Approval depends on workload and business needs.

### CARRYOVER
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year.  
Additional unused days expire unless law requires otherwise.

---

## 6.2 SICK LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per year.  
Additional leave may be granted depending on local laws.

### NOTIFICATION
Employees must:
- notify their manager as soon as possible  
- provide a medical certificate for absences exceeding two days or when required  

Employees should not work while ill unless fully able.

---

## 6.3 MEDICAL LEAVE
Employees with serious illnesses, injuries, or long-term conditions may request extended medical leave.

Requirements:
- medical documentation  
- HR approval  
- compliance with regional regulations  

Return-to-work plans may be implemented.

---

## 6.4 PARENTAL LEAVE

### PRIMARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave.

### SECONDARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
Secondary caregivers receive 2 weeks of paid leave.

Additional unpaid leave may be approved according to local law.

Employees must notify HR at least eight weeks before the anticipated leave date.

---

## 6.5 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees may take 3 paid days of leave due to the death of an immediate family member.

Immediate family includes:
- spouse or partner  
- parent  
- child  
- sibling  
- grandparent  

Additional unpaid leave may be granted based on circumstances.

---

## 6.6 PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Employees receive paid public holidays according to their region.

Some employees may work on holidays due to client needs.  
They may receive compensatory time off or additional pay as applicable.

---

## 6.7 SPECIAL LEAVE
Special leave may include:
- jury duty  
- court appearances  
- voting leave  
- emergency personal leave  
- unpaid leave for extended personal matters  

Requests are reviewed based on business needs and performance history.

---

## 6.8 LONG SERVICE LEAVE
Where required by law, long service leave entitlements will be honored.  
Eligibility depends on regional regulations.

---

## 6.9 LEAVE TRACKING AND DOCUMENTATION
Employees must:
- submit accurate leave requests  
- maintain up-to-date records  
- provide required documentation when necessary  

Misuse of leave may result in disciplinary action.

# PART 8: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES AND GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION
---

## 8.1 OVERVIEW
Disciplinary and grievance procedures ensure fair, consistent handling of behavioral issues, performance concerns, and workplace conflicts.  
Procedures exist to maintain professional standards and a safe working environment.

The company may bypass steps in cases of serious misconduct.

---

## 8.2 MISCONDUCT
Misconduct includes behavior that violates company policy or workplace standards.

Examples:
- repeated tardiness  
- unprofessional conduct  
- failure to follow instructions  
- misuse of company property  
- disregard for safety protocols  

Misconduct is normally handled through progressive discipline.

---

## 8.3 SERIOUS MISCONDUCT
Serious misconduct can result in immediate suspension or termination.

Examples:
- harassment or discrimination  
- theft or fraud  
- confidentiality breaches  
- workplace violence or threats  
- intoxication at work  
- intentional safety violations  
- falsifying documents  
- gross insubordination  

---

## 8.4 PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PROCESS

### STEP 1: VERBAL WARNING
A verbal discussion outlining concerns and expectations.

### STEP 2: WRITTEN WARNING
A documented warning including:
- nature of issue  
- required improvements  
- timeline for correction  

### STEP 3: FINAL WARNING
Issued when issues persist or escalate.

### STEP 4: TERMINATION
Employment may be terminated if no improvement is shown.

The company may adjust the sequence based on severity.

---

## 8.5 PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
Used for ongoing performance issues.

A PIP includes:
- specific performance concerns  
- defined improvement goals  
- timeline (typically 30–60 days)  
- scheduled check-ins  
- performance metrics  

Failure to meet PIP expectations may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 8.6 SUSPENSION
Employees may be suspended (paid or unpaid depending on law) during investigations into:
- misconduct  
- safety incidents  
- conflicts requiring fact-finding  

Suspension does not imply guilt.

---

## 8.7 WORKPLACE INVESTIGATIONS
Investigations will:
- maintain confidentiality where possible  
- treat all parties fairly  
- gather evidence and statements  
- determine findings based on facts  

Employees must cooperate fully.

---

## 8.8 GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

### STEP 1: INFORMAL RESOLUTION
Employees should first attempt to resolve issues by discussing concerns with their manager.

### STEP 2: FORMAL GRIEVANCE
If unresolved, a written grievance must be submitted to HR including:
- description of concern  
- individuals involved  
- prior attempts to resolve  
- desired resolution  

### STEP 3: REVIEW AND MEETING
HR reviews information and may arrange meetings with involved parties.

### STEP 4: DECISION
HR issues a written decision.  
Additional steps may include mediation or corrective action.

---

## 8.9 CONFLICT RESOLUTION
Employees should:
- address issues early  
- communicate respectfully  
- seek assistance from managers or HR  
- participate in mediation when required  

Managers must intervene when conflicts escalate.

---

## 8.10 NON-RETALIATION
Retaliation against employees who report misconduct or participate in investigations is prohibited.  
Violations result in disciplinary action.

---

## 8.11 RECORDKEEPING
HR securely stores disciplinary records and grievance documentation according to privacy laws.

---
