# 1. PARTIES
This Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is made between:

**Company:** BluePeak Creative Group (“Company”)  
**Employee / Contractor:** __________________________________ (“Recipient”)  

Together referred to as “the Parties.”

---

# 2. PURPOSE
The Company agrees to share certain confidential information with the Recipient for the purpose of performing employment duties or project-related tasks.

The Recipient agrees to protect and not disclose such information except as allowed under this Agreement.

---

# 3. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
“Confidential Information” includes, but is not limited to:

- client lists and client data  
- marketing strategies and plans  
- financial information  
- internal documents and procedures  
- creative materials, drafts, concepts, ideas  
- prototypes, designs, and software  
- employee information  
- business forecasts and strategies  
- intellectual property belonging to the Company  
- any non-public information shared verbally, digitally, or in writing  

Confidential Information remains the property of the Company.

---

# 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE EMPLOYEE
The Recipient agrees to:

- keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential  
- not disclose information to any third party  
- use information only for work-related purposes  
- store information securely  
- avoid unauthorized copying, forwarding, or distribution  
- comply with all Company data protection policies  
- notify the Company immediately in case of suspected data breach  

These obligations apply during and after employment.

---

# 5. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that:

- is publicly available through no fault of the Recipient  
- was already in the Recipient’s possession before disclosure  
- is independently developed without use of Company information  
- is lawfully obtained from a third party  
- must be disclosed under legal obligation (with prior notice to the Company, if possible)

---

# 6. PERMITTED DISCLOSURE
Disclosure is permitted only when:

- expressly approved in writing by the Company  
- required for the Recipient’s official work duties  
- required by law or court order (Company must be notified)

---

# 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All materials, concepts, designs, strategies, and creative or technical outputs produced during the Recipient’s engagement:

- are the exclusive property of BluePeak Creative Group  
- must not be shared or reused without written consent  
- remain owned by the Company after employment ends  

---

# 8. DATA PROTECTION
The Recipient must comply with:

- Company data protection rules  
- GDPR or other applicable laws  
- IT and cybersecurity policies  
- secure handling of digital and physical data  

---

# 9. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon termination of employment or upon request, the Recipient must return:

- all documents  
- devices  
- access cards  
- software and digital files  
- prototypes or creative materials  
- any other Company property  

No copies may be retained in any form.

---

# 10. TERM OF AGREEMENT
This Agreement:

- begins on the date of signing  
- remains in effect throughout employment  
- continues indefinitely after employment ends  
- has no expiration for trade secrets  

---

# 11. REMEDIES
If the Recipient breaches this Agreement, the Company may seek:

- injunctive relief  
- financial damages  
- recovery of legal costs  
- termination of employment  

The Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm.

---

# 12. NO LICENSE GRANTED
Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any rights to:

- intellectual property  
- trade secrets  
- proprietary tools or materials  

except as needed to perform assigned duties.

---

# 13. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which BluePeak Creative Group operates.
