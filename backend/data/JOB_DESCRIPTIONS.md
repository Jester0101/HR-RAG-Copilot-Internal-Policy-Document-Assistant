# JD 1: GRAPHIC DESIGNER

## POSITION SUMMARY
The Graphic Designer creates visual concepts, brand assets, digital materials, and campaign graphics for BluePeak Creative Group and its clients.

## RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design digital and print materials  
- Develop brand identity assets  
- Create social media content and visuals  
- Collaborate with creative and marketing teams  
- Ensure all designs follow brand guidelines  
- Prepare files for production and delivery  
- Participate in brainstorming and creative development  

## REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor’s degree in design or related field  
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite  
- Strong portfolio demonstrating design skills  
- Knowledge of layout, color theory, typography  
- Ability to work under deadlines  

## KPIs
- Quality of visual output  
- Timely delivery of materials  
- Client satisfaction  
- Compliance with brand standards

# JD 2: MARKETING SPECIALIST

## POSITION SUMMARY
The Marketing Specialist supports campaign planning, execution, analytics, and content development.

## RESPONSIBILITIES
- Plan and execute marketing campaigns  
- Conduct competitor and market research  
- Manage social media calendars  
- Coordinate with creative teams  
- Produce marketing performance reports  
- Support SEO and email marketing campaigns  

## REQUIREMENTS
- Degree in marketing or related field  
- Strong analytical and communication skills  
- Familiarity with digital marketing tools  
- Ability to manage multiple projects  

## KPIs
- Campaign performance metrics  
- Growth in audience engagement  
- Lead generation and conversion rates

# JD 3: COPYWRITER

## POSITION SUMMARY
The Copywriter creates written content for campaigns, brand messaging, websites, social media, and client materials.

## RESPONSIBILITIES
- Write clear and engaging copy  
- Develop brand voice and messaging  
- Collaborate with designers and strategists  
- Edit and proofread content  
- Create scripts for video and audio content  
- Research target audiences and trends  

## REQUIREMENTS
- Excellent writing skills  
- Experience in marketing or creative writing  
- Ability to adapt tone for different audiences  
- Strong editing and grammar knowledge  

## KPIs
- Quality and creativity of content  
- Engagement metrics  
- Timeliness of deliverables
