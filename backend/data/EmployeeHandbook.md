# PART 1: WELCOME
---

## 1.1 MESSAGE FROM LEADERSHIP
Welcome to BluePeak Creative Group. We are a global creative agency focused on delivering meaningful work through collaboration, innovation, and excellence.  
This handbook is designed to help you understand how we work, what we expect, and how you can succeed here.

## 1.2 ABOUT BLUEPEAK
BluePeak Creative Group specializes in:
- brand strategy  
- digital content development  
- marketing consulting  
- multimedia production  

We believe creativity works best when paired with strong communication, teamwork, and accountability.

## 1.3 OUR VALUES
- **Creativity** — we challenge ideas and explore new concepts  
- **Integrity** — we act with honesty and professionalism  
- **Collaboration** — we work together to achieve exceptional results  
- **Responsibility** — we own our work and outcomes  
- **Growth** — we constantly learn and evolve

# PART 2: WORKING AT BLUEPEAK
---

## 2.1 EMPLOYMENT BASICS
Your job offer outlines:
- position  
- department  
- compensation  
- employment type  
- manager  
- start date  

Employees must review and follow all company policies located in the HR Manual.

## 2.2 WORK SCHEDULE
Standard work hours are 40 hours per week.  
Your manager will confirm your schedule.  
Flexibility may be approved depending on role and workload.

## 2.3 OFFICE ETIQUETTE
Employees are expected to:
- maintain a clean workspace  
- be respectful in shared areas  
- limit noise  
- follow meeting room rules  
- treat colleagues with professionalism  

## 2.4 REMOTE WORK EXPECTATIONS
Remote employees must:
- be reachable during work hours  
- attend all meetings  
- maintain a secure workspace  
- follow data security standards  

---

# PART 3: POLICIES YOU SHOULD KNOW
---

## 3.1 CODE OF CONDUCT SUMMARY
Employees must:
- act ethically  
- follow company policies  
- maintain professionalism  
- avoid conflicts of interest  
- uphold respectful communication  

## 3.2 ANTI-HARASSMENT
Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.  
Employees must report inappropriate behavior to HR immediately.

## 3.3 DATA SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must:
- protect confidential documents  
- follow password rules  
- avoid sharing sensitive information  
- use company-approved platforms only  

All internal and client data must remain secure at all times.

## 3.4 USE OF COMPANY EQUIPMENT
Employees must take care of:
- laptops  
- mobile devices  
- creative equipment  
- access cards  

Equipment may only be used for authorized work purposes.

---

# PART 4: TIME OFF AND BENEFITS
---

## 4.1 PTO OVERVIEW
Employees receive Paid Time Off according to the HR Manual.  
PTO must be requested in advance and approved by the manager.

## 4.2 SICK LEAVE
If you are ill:
- notify your manager as early as possible  
- stay home and rest  
- submit medical documents when required  

## 4.3 PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Employees receive all regionally recognized public holidays.  
Some roles may require holiday work; compensatory time will be provided.

## 4.4 PARENTAL LEAVE
BluePeak offers paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers.  
Details are outlined in the HR Manual.

---

# PART 5: PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT
---

## 5.1 PROBATION PERIOD
The standard probation period is three months.  
Check-ins will be held to assess:
- performance  
- communication  
- adaptability  
- teamwork  

## 5.2 PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Employees receive periodic performance reviews focusing on:
- quality of work  
- deadlines  
- collaboration  
- initiative  
- growth potential  

## 5.3 LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
BluePeak encourages continuous learning through:
- workshops  
- training sessions  
- mentorship  
- skill development resources  

Employees are expected to actively pursue professional growth.

---

# PART 6: SAFETY AND WELL-BEING
---

## 6.1 HEALTH AND SAFETY
Employees must follow all health and safety guidelines.  
Work areas should remain clean and free of hazards.

## 6.2 REPORTING INCIDENTS
Report all incidents immediately to:
- your manager  
- HR  
- Health & Safety representatives  

Accurate documentation is required for investigations.

## 6.3 MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS
BluePeak promotes well-being through optional wellness resources.  
Employees are encouraged to request support when needed.

---

# PART 7: WHEN YOU LEAVE BLUEPEAK
---

## 7.1 RESIGNATION PROCESS
Employees must:
- submit a written resignation  
- provide required notice  
- work with their manager on handover plans  

## 7.2 FINAL PAY AND EQUIPMENT RETURN
Before departure, employees must return:
- devices  
- access cards  
- documents  
- any company property  

Final compensation will follow local regulations and internal policy.
