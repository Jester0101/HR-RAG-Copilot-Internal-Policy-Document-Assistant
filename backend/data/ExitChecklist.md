# 1. NOTICE & DOCUMENTATION
- EMPLOYEE SUBMITS WRITTEN RESIGNATION OR RECEIVES NOTICE OF TERMINATION  
- HR ACKNOWLEDGES RECEIPT OF NOTICE  
- CONFIRM LAST WORKING DAY  
- CONFIRM REASON FOR SEPARATION (RESIGNATION / TERMINATION / REDUNDANCY)  
- RETRIEVE AND FILE ALL REQUIRED DOCUMENTS  
- VERIFY EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT TERMS REGARDING NOTICE PERIOD  

---

# 2. HR PROCESSING
- UPDATE HR SYSTEMS WITH TERMINATION DATE  
- REVIEW EMPLOYEE’S LEAVE BALANCES  
- PREPARE REQUIRED EXIT DOCUMENTS  
- SCHEDULE EXIT INTERVIEW  
- NOTIFY PAYROLL, IT, AND RELEVANT DEPARTMENTS  
- CONFIRM WHETHER EMPLOYEE IS ELIGIBLE FOR REHIRE  

---

# 3. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
- PREPARE TRANSITION PLAN FOR PROJECTS  
- IDENTIFY WHO WILL TAKE OVER TASKS  
- COLLECT INFORMATION ON OUTSTANDING DELIVERABLES  
- CONDUCT PERFORMANCE SUMMARY (OPTIONAL)  
- COMMUNICATE DEPARTURE TO THE TEAM (WITH HR APPROVAL)  

---

# 4. IT OFFBOARDING
- DISABLE ACCESS TO COMPANY SYSTEMS (SCHEDULED FOR LAST WORKING DAY)  
  - Email  
  - Slack / Teams  
  - Project Management Tools  
  - File Storage Platforms  
  - VPN Access  
- BACK UP NECESSARY COMPANY DATA  
- TRANSFER FILE OWNERSHIP TO MANAGER  
- REMOVE ACCESS TO CONFIDENTIAL FOLDERS  
- CHECK SOFTWARE LICENSES AND FREE SEATS  

---

# 5. RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY
The employee must return all company-owned items:

- Laptop & Charger  
- Monitor(s)  
- Keyboard & Mouse  
- Mobile Phone (If issued)  
- Access Cards & Keys  
- Headphones or Audio Equipment  
- Company Credit Card  
- Physical Documents or Notebooks  
- Any Creative or Production Equipment  
- Uniform / Branded Items (If applicable)  

Manager and HR must verify all items are returned.

---

# 6. FINAL PAY & BENEFITS
- CONFIRM PAYMENT OF:  
  - Last salary  
  - Overtime (if applicable)  
  - Unused PTO (if paid out)  
  - Outstanding reimbursements  

- REVIEW BENEFITS STATUS:  
  - End of insurance coverage  
  - End of other benefits  

- PREPARE FINAL PAYSLIP  

---

# 7. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
- HANDOVER DOCUMENT CREATED  
- CURRENT PROJECT STATUS UPDATED  
- FILES AND DOCUMENTS TRANSFERRED TO TEAM  
- CLIENT COMMUNICATION TRANSITIONED  
- PASSWORDS STORED IN COMPANY-APPROVED SYSTEM (IF APPLICABLE)  

---

# 8. EXIT INTERVIEW
Conducted by HR:

- DISCUSS REASON FOR LEAVING  
- COLLECT FEEDBACK ON:  
  - Role  
  - Manager  
  - Team  
  - Company culture  
  - Suggestions for improvement  
- DISCUSS POST-EMPLOYMENT OBLIGATIONS  
- CONFIRM CONTACT INFORMATION  

---

# 9. ACCOUNT CLOSURE & COMPLIANCE
- CLOSE PAYROLL PROFILE  
- DEACTIVATE HR SYSTEM ACCESS  
- ARCHIVE EMPLOYEE FILE  
- STORE EXIT INTERVIEW NOTES  
- DOCUMENT DEVICE & PROPERTY RETURN  
- FINALIZE TERMINATION RECORD  

---

# 10. FINAL DAY PROCEDURES
- MANAGER MEETS WITH EMPLOYEE  
- FINAL GOODBYE TO TEAM (OPTIONAL)  
- RETURN OF LAST ITEMS  
- CONFIRM LAST TIME ENTRY  
- HR CONFIRMS COMPLETION OF ALL CHECKLIST ITEMS  

---

# 11. POST-EMPLOYMENT OBLIGATIONS
Employee must comply with:

- CONFIDENTIALITY REQUIREMENTS (NDA REMAINS IN EFFECT)  
- RETURN OF ANY ADDITIONAL MATERIALS AFTER EXIT  
- NON-COMPETE CLAUSE (IF APPLICABLE)  
- RESTRICTIONS ON CONTACTING CLIENTS (IF APPLICABLE)
