# PRE-START ACTIONS
Before the employee arrives, the following tasks must be completed by HR, IT, and the Hiring Manager:

- OFFER LETTER SENT AND SIGNED  
- EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT SIGNED  
- NDA SIGNED  
- PERSONAL INFORMATION FORM SUBMITTED  
- WORKSTATION ASSIGNED  
- IT EQUIPMENT PREPARED  
  - Laptop  
  - Charger  
  - Monitor(s)  
  - Keyboard & Mouse  
- SOFTWARE ACCOUNTS CREATED  
  - Email  
  - Slack / Teams  
  - Project management tools  
  - File storage platforms  
- ACCESS PERMISSIONS ASSIGNED  
  - Office access card  
  - Network access  
  - Shared folder permissions  
- WELCOME PACKAGE PREPARED  
  - Employee handbook  
  - HR policies  
  - Organizational chart  
  - Role description (JD)  
- MANAGER PREPARES 30-DAY PLAN

# FIRST DAY CHECKLIST
Employee arrives and completes the essential onboarding steps:

- GREETING AND OFFICE TOUR  
- INTRODUCTION TO TEAM MEMBERS  
- HR ORIENTATION SESSION  
  - Overview of company values and culture  
  - Review of HR policies  
  - Overview of working hours & attendance  
- REVIEW OF ROLE EXPECTATIONS  
- IT SETUP  
  - Login to email  
  - Connect to Wi-Fi  
  - Access project management platforms  
  - Password setup and security settings  
- WORKSTATION SETUP  
- SIGN REMAINING DOCUMENTS (IF ANY)  
- MANAGER 1:1 MEETING  
  - Clarify responsibilities  
  - First-day tasks assigned  
- LUNCH WITH TEAM (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)

---

# FIRST WEEK CHECKLIST
The goal is to integrate the employee into ongoing projects and workflows.

- REVIEW DETAILED JOB RESPONSIBILITIES  
- INTRODUCTION TO ONGOING PROJECTS  
- TRAINING ON KEY SOFTWARE TOOLS  
- SETUP OF PROJECT ACCESS  
- MEETING WITH CROSS-FUNCTIONAL TEAMS  
- SAFETY AND SECURITY TRAINING  
- COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES REVIEW  
- ASSIGN FIRST PROJECT TASKS  
- DAILY CHECK-INS WITH MANAGER  
- HR FOLLOW-UP ON QUESTIONS  

---

# FIRST MONTH CHECKLIST
The employee should now be functioning independently and contributing actively.

- PERFORMANCE CHECK-IN WITH MANAGER (WEEK 2)  
- VERIFY ACCESS NEEDS AND ADJUST PERMISSIONS  
- WORK QUALITY REVIEW  
- FEEDBACK SESSION (EMPLOYEE → MANAGER)  
- FEEDBACK SESSION (MANAGER → EMPLOYEE)  
- TEAM INTEGRATION CHECK  
- ADVANCED TRAINING SESSIONS  
  - Department tools  
  - Company processes  
- UPDATE 30-DAY PLAN → 60-DAY PLAN  

---

# PROBATION PERIOD CHECKLIST
The standard probation period is 3 months.

- 30-DAY REVIEW COMPLETED  
- 60-DAY REVIEW COMPLETED  
- PERFORMANCE EVALUATION FORM COMPLETED  
- DISCUSSION OF IMPROVEMENT AREAS  
- TRAINING OR SUPPORT PLAN (IF REQUIRED)  
- FINAL PROBATION MEETING  
- DECISION:  
  - CONFIRM EMPLOYMENT  
  - EXTEND PROBATION (IF NECESSARY)  
  - TERMINATE EMPLOYMENT (IF NOT A FIT)
